Choir September 2026

«Velsigne dette land»

An earthbound, gently spiritual choral piece from the opera Marko og Adriane, well suited as an accessible concert work.

«Velsigne dette land» (Bless This Land) is an intimate choral setting for SATB choir and piano, originating from the opera "Marko og Adriane".

Characterized by melodic warmth, flowing voice leading, and an unpretentious, contemplative spirit, the piece offers a serene blessing over nature, life, and community. With its accessible vocal range and gentle tempo, it works equally well as an evocative concert piece or an anthem of peace.
